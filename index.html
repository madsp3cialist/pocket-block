How to play

Grab the ball or its halo and pull it back from the fixed tether point. Release to fly over the blocks and land on a target. The landing spot is the mirror of the release spot across the tether: pull farther down to land farther up; pull right to land left. Use the original board grid to judge the distance. The ball always returns to the same resting spot. Tapping elsewhere does not move it. A tap or an interrupted touch cancels.

Only the landing cell is hit. Blocks underneath the flight path are passed over. Land on any block to clear its connected color group. Bigger groups earn more. A flashing block turns the remaining board into its displayed color when hit. A numbered bomb clears a 5 × 5 area when hit. Both special types count down from 9; if time runs out, they become ordinary blocks. New blocks fall into the stacks. If a stack crosses the dashed top line, the game ends.

Classic adds blocks on a timer. Relaxed adds them after every three shots. Your best score is saved separately for each mode on this device when browser storage is available. Switching away automatically pauses.

Keyboard: ← → aim, ↑ ↓ change power, Space shoots, P pauses. Sound is optional. In Safari, use Share → Add to Home Screen for an app-like shortcut.
